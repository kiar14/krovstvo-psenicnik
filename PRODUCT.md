# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 + GSAP/Lenis/Motion, deployed on Vercel (per the agency's `premium-web-stack` standard). Demo phase: no database, contact form only shows a success state.

## Users

- **Homeowners in Koroška** (Dravograd, Libeliče, Otiški vrh, Ravne na Koroškem, Vuzenica, Prevalje) who need a new roof, a re-roof, gutters/flashing, roof windows, a carport or a lightning conductor. Often older family houses; the decision is expensive and rare, so they are cautious and compare 2–3 contractors.
- **Austrian customers** across the border (Carinthia); the site offers a German version.
- Secondary: farms, businesses, parishes and apartment-block managers (farm buildings, flat roofs, churches, lightning conductors).

Their job: find a contractor they can trust to show up, do it properly and finish on the agreed date, then call or send an inquiry.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for **PŠENIČNIK storitve d.o.o.**, a roofing, tinsmithing and carpentry contractor in Libeliče. It must turn a visitor into a phone call (041 757 179) or an inquiry, by proving craft and reliability. Success = more qualified inquiries from the service area and Austria.

## Positioning

A **master tinsmith-roofer** (Tomo Pšeničnik, OZS master's exam) runs every job himself. All three trades (krovstvo, kleparstvo, tesarstvo) plus roof windows, lightning conductors with certificate and work at height come from one contractor, from the site visit to the finished roof. Promise: "Hitro, kvalitetno in v dogovorjenih rokih."

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly arrive on phones, from Google search ("krovec Dravograd", "menjava strehe Koroška") or a recommendation. The process they expect: site visit → measuring → advice on materials → written quote → work. They choose from ~30 roofing brands (Tondach, Bramac, Decra, Prefa, Creaton…).

## Product Principles

1. Reliability first: every section should make "they show up and finish on time" more believable.
2. Show the craft: close-ups of real detail work beat generic roof stock.
3. One call away: phone and inquiry reachable from every screen, especially on mobile.
4. Local and personal: a family master craftsman from Libeliče, not a faceless firm.
5. Honest: no invented numbers or praise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many visitors are older homeowners: large readable type, strong contrast, big tap targets, reduced-motion fallback for every animation.
