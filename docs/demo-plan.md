## Visual direction (choose one)

The design roll ran without outside challengers (the service couldn't be reached from this environment). These directions come from the business's own world.

### A · "Ostrešje" (the roof structure): assigned direction
- **World:** the carpenter's and tinsmith's workshop. The page is built like a roof structure: rafter lines at the real roof pitch (~40°) divide sections, carpenter's dimension lines measure photos, and chalk-line strokes snap across as sections arrive.
- **Palette (committed):** heritage **navy `#002D8A`** as the large blue-chalk field (trust bar, process, contact), **fresh spruce `#D9A864`** for timber accents, **terracotta `#BA3201`** only for actions (call, send), **graphite `#1C2126`** for text, on a cool **cement-grey `#EEF0F1`** base.
- **Type:** a condensed, structural display face (e.g. Big Shoulders Display) for headlines and measurements; **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for body text, chosen for older readers.
- **Why it fits:** it shows craft and precision, keeps the old site's navy and terracotta recognisable, and none of the local competitors look like this.
- **Honest risk:** the most distinctive option. Timber tones could feel "carpentry only" if used too much; the photos of real roofs must stay the hero.

### B · "Detajl" (construction detail sheet): my own top pick
- **World:** a roofer's technical detail drawing. A clean light page, fine navy hairlines, numbered callouts with leader lines pointing into real photos (1 tile, 2 batten, 3 membrane, 4 gutter), and section hatching as texture.
- **Palette (restrained):** white and cement-grey, navy lines, terracotta for actions only.
- **Why it fits:** it shows expertise and explains the work.
- **Honest risk:** familiar ("blueprint" looks are common in construction), cooler and less local and personal.

### C · Standard roofer site (the safe option)
A full-bleed drone photo in the hero, white cards, navy and orange buttons. Played straight and done well, but it looks like everyone else.

## Signature moments (choose one; my recommendation first)

### ① "Streha nastane" (the roof builds itself): **recommended**
- **What the visitor sees:** a pinned section. As you scroll, one roof is built layer by layer: bare timber rafters → membrane → battens → tiles → gutters and flashing → a roof window. At each layer a short line names the trade that does it (tesarstvo → krovstvo → kleparstvo → strešna okna). It ends on the finished roof in evening light, with the call button.
- **Why:** it shows the one thing that makes them different: all three trades, one contractor, start to finish.
- **Needs from you:** 5 AI keyframes (the same camera, each an edit of the previous one) and one 8–10 s AI video from them. Prompts come in `asset-plan.md`.
- **Build:** a scroll-scrubbed WebP frame sequence on a canvas. On mobile a lighter sequence, and with reduced motion a still of the finished roof.
- **Effort:** medium.

### ② "Staro → novo" (old → new)
- **What the visitor sees:** a typical Koroška house with a tired grey fibre-cement roof. A vertical line sweeps across as you scroll (or you drag it) and reveals the same house with a new red tile roof, new gutters and roof windows.
- **Why:** re-roofing is their most common job, and it's easy to understand.
- **Needs from you:** 2 AI images (the "after" made as an edit of the "before"). Marked "ilustracija" (illustration), because it isn't their own project.
- **Effort:** easy.

### ③ "Pot vode" (the path of the rain)
- **What the visitor sees:** a gutter and downpipe line (their tinwork) runs down the whole page and draws itself as you scroll. Each bend is a station in the process section, and a drop of water runs along it.
- **Why:** it turns their kleparstvo into the page's guide line.
- **Needs from you:** nothing (built in SVG).
- **Effort:** easy to medium.

**Recommended combination:** ① as the one hero moment (section 4), with ③ used quietly in the process section only (section 5). Everything else stays calm (fades, light parallax).
