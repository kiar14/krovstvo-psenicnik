## Visual direction: A · "Ostrešje" (chosen)

- **World:** the carpenter's and tinsmith's workshop. The page is built like a roof structure: rafter lines at the real roof pitch (~40°) divide sections, carpenter's dimension lines measure photos (e.g. the hero), and chalk-line strokes snap across as sections arrive. Real roof photos stay the hero; timber is an accent, not a theme.
- **Palette (committed):** heritage **navy `#002D8A`** as the large blue-chalk field (trust bar, process, contact), **fresh spruce `#D9A864`** for timber accents and dimension lines, **terracotta `#BA3201`** only for actions (call, send), **graphite `#1C2126`** for text, on a cool **cement-grey `#EEF0F1`** base with white cards.
- **Type:** a condensed structural display face (Big Shoulders Display; it covers č, š, ž and umlauts) for headlines, numbers and measurements; **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for body text (chosen for older readers).
- **Motion:** calm by default (fade and rise, light parallax on photos, chalk-line snaps under headings). One loud moment only: Staro → novo.
- **Mobile first:** the call button is always reachable, with large tap targets.

## Signature moment: ② "Staro → novo" (chosen)

- **Where:** section 4, right after the services, so it proves the Krovstvo card.
- **What the visitor sees:** one typical Koroška house fills the screen with a tired grey fibre-cement roof, moss, rusty gutters. As you scroll, the section pins and a thin chalk line (spruce colour, with a small handle) sweeps from left to right, revealing the same house with a new terracotta tile roof, anthracite gutters, two roof windows and snow guards.
- **Text beats (on the left, changing with the sweep):**
  1. "Pred: dotrajana kritina, zamašeni žlebovi" (before: worn-out roofing, blocked gutters)
  2. "Menjava kritine, žlebov in obrob" (new roofing, gutters and flashing)
  3. "Po: ena ekipa, en rok, nova streha" (after: one team, one deadline, a new roof), with the button "Pošljite povpraševanje" (send an inquiry)
- **After the pin:** the handle stays draggable (mouse, touch and keyboard arrows).
- **Mobile:** the same, in a portrait crop (4:5).
- **Reduced motion:** a side-by-side still pair.
- **Label:** a small "ilustracija" tag in the corner, since it isn't their own project. When they send a real before/after pair from the same spot, we swap it in and drop the label.
- **Assets:** 2 AI images; prompts in `docs/asset-plan.md`.
